About this GTU SPI Calculator

This is a free GTU SPI calculator for Gujarat Technological University students. Enter your marks or grades for each subject and it shows your semester SPI. Set a target SPI and it tells you the ESE marks you still need. Templates are available for CE Semester 1 to 5, and Custom works for any branch.

How GTU SPI is calculated

SPI = Σ(Grade Point × Credits) ÷ Σ Credits

Grade points: AA = 10, AB = 9, BB = 8, BC = 7, CC = 6, CD = 5, DD = 4, FF = 0. Only the final subject grade becomes a grade point. Subjects with more credits count more.

FAQ

What is the difference between SPI and CGPA?
SPI is your result for one semester. CGPA is the cumulative result across all semesters.

How do I find the marks needed for my target SPI?
Switch to Expected SPI, set your target, enter the marks you already have, and open the pending subject. The calculator shows the marks needed in the GTU exam.

Is my data stored online?
No. Everything is calculated and saved in your own browser.

Is this an official GTU tool?
No. It is an independent student project. Required-mark results are estimates, not an official GTU guarantee.
